About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Weierstraß Institute, working in the group of Prof. Michael Hintermüller. My research focuses on analysis of PDEs and optimization and several applications, including nonlinear acoustics, fluid dynamics, finance, data science. I am particularly interested in developing and analyzing mathematical models and computational methods that can address complex problems in these areas.

Alongside my academic work, I am actively exploring opportunities in data science and quantitative finance, where I can apply rigorous statistical and computational methods to real-world problems.

Before my current position, I was a PhD student at the University of Memphis, where I worked on singular perturbation problems and boundary stabilization of nonlinear acoustic waves and applications in high intensity focused ultrasound procedures. My doctoral research provided me with a strong foundation in the analysis, stabilization and control of hyperbolic PDEs, which I continue to build upon in my postdoctoral work.
