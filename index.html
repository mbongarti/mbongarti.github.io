About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Weierstraß Institute, working in the group of Prof. Michael Hintermüller. My research lies in the analysis of PDEs and optimization, with applications spanning nonlinear acoustics, fluid dynamics, finance, and data science. Lately I have grown particularly interested in using game theory to model strategic interactions in these domains — studying their mathematical structure and developing computational methods to solve them.

Alongside my academic work, I am actively exploring opportunities in data science and quantitative finance, where I can apply rigorous statistical and computational methods to real-world problems.

Before my current position, I was a PhD student at the University of Memphis, working under Irena Lasiecka and Roberto Triggiani, where I worked on singular perturbation problems and boundary stabilization of nonlinear acoustic waves and applications in high intensity focused ultrasound procedures. My doctoral research provided me with a strong foundation in the analysis, stabilization and control of hyperbolic PDEs, which I continue to build upon in my postdoctoral work.
